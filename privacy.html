Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Orbital Shipyard Publisher is an internal tool of the Orbital Shipyard art studio (Mikołaj Pyka, Poland). It publishes our own artwork to our own accounts: short videos to our YouTube channel and image Pins to our Pinterest business account, and it reads basic information about those accounts. It is used only by the studio itself; it is not offered to other users.

Data we access

With the studio's own consent, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload our videos, manage our own playlists and comments, and read our channel name and video statistics. We do not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube users.

On Pinterest, with the studio's own consent (OAuth 2.0), the tool uses the Pinterest API to list our own boards, create Pins with images of our own artwork that link to our own shop listings, and read statistics of our own Pins. We do not access, collect or store data about any other Pinterest users.

Storage and sharing

The only stored credentials are the studio's own OAuth refresh tokens (Google and Pinterest), kept encrypted. No personal data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Pinterest access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest settings under Security and logins / Apps. Our use of the Pinterest API follows the Pinterest Developer Guidelines. Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
