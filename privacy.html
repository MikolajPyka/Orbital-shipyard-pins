Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Orbital Shipyard Publisher is an internal tool of the Orbital Shipyard art studio (Mikołaj Pyka, Poland). It publishes short videos of our own artwork to our own YouTube channel and reads basic information about that channel. It is used only by the studio itself; it is not offered to other users.

Data we access

With the studio's own consent, the tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload our videos, manage our own playlists and comments, and read our channel name and video statistics. We do not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube users.

Storage and sharing

The only stored credential is the studio's own OAuth refresh token, kept encrypted. No personal data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
