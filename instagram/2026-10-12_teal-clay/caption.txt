Same city, different light. 🌇

1. Destroyer KV-7840 has been hanging over the old copper district for nine days, engines idling, hatches sealed. The city below has started setting its clocks by her shadow. Nobody has asked what she is waiting for.

2. Mothership MTR-5867 was built in the middle of the city, one district at a time, while people went on living around her. Tonight she is lighting up for the first time. Half the city has gone up onto the roofs to watch.

This is a new palette for the shipyard: teal and clay. I start the story. You get to finish it.

Which ship would you follow, and where does she go next? 👇
