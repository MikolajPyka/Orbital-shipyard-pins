Welcome to the Orbital Shipyard. 🚀

Five ships, one dry dock, five unfinished stories:

1. Destroyer KV-7930 has sat in dry dock for 31 days while engineers argue over a single sealed compartment. The blueprints say it is empty. The thermal scans disagree.
2. Frigate DR-2288 was supposed to be scrapped last winter. Yet every night her reactor warms up in Hangar 7.
3. Destroyer RX-0725 came home to the moon Hilon after four years on patrol. Half her hull plates were new. None of the crew remembered the battle that cost the old ones.
4. Destroyer DR-4648 docked with one engine dead. The ship's log ends mid-sentence.
5. In the deepest bay, Destroyer SK-7569 is getting a name painted on her bow for the first time. Nobody knows whose.

Every ship here is built by my own procedural generator, written in code, so no two vessels are alike. Each one is the start of a story, and the rest is yours to write.

Which ship would you board? Tell us in the comments. 👇
