Procedural sci-fi art: mothership MTR-6847 assembled module by module in an orbital dock. 🏗️

The dock crew built MTR-6847 from four hulls that were never meant to fly together: a survey ship, two freighters and the spine of a decommissioned carrier. The welds held on the first pressure test. Then the old carrier's beacon switched itself back on and started broadcasting a call sign nobody in the yard recognises.
