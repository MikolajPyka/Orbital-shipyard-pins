Same ship. Eight different nights. 🎨

KV-1770 has flown the same corridor over Halvor-9 every night for eleven years. The city changes its lights. She never changes her route.

The pilots say the towers remember her colours. Some nights they glow amber, some nights teal, and nobody on the ground can agree which one is real.

I start the story. You get to finish it. Which night would you fly her through? 👇
